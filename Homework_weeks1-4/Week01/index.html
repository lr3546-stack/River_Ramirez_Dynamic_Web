Slow Cooked Rosemary Lamb Ragu

Lamb shoulder slow cooked in a red wine and tomato sauce with rosemary until it falls apart, then shredded and tossed through pappardelle.

Ingredients

  • 1.25 kg boneless lamb shoulder, cut into 3 pieces
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 celery sticks, grated
  • 1 large carrot, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp rosemary, finely chopped, plus 5 sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 cups red wine
  • 400 g canned crushed tomato
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 beef stock cubes, crumbled
  • 500 g pappardelle
  • Parmesan, for serving

Instructions

  1. Season the lamb with salt and pepper and brown it in olive oil, then set it aside.
  2. Cook the onion, celery, carrot, garlic and chopped rosemary slowly for 7 - 8 minutes.
  3. Cook the tomato paste for 1 1/2 minutes, then add the wine and reduce by half.
  4. Add the tomato, stock, stock cubes, bay leaves, 3 rosemary sprigs and the lamb.
  5. Cover and simmer gently for 2 hours, until the lamb shreds easily.
  6. Shred the lamb, return it to the pot and simmer uncovered for 30 minutes.
  7. Cook the pasta 1 minute short of the packet time and save some pasta water.
  8. Toss the pasta with the ragu and a splash of pasta water, then serve with parmesan.
